---
name: find-practical-help
description: "Use when someone needs food, help with a utility bill, or a short referral plan spanning more than one need."
---

Use `find_community_help` for a practical need or `find_bill_assistance` for the specific bill category. Use `plan_referrals` when two or more needs could lead to different agencies. Ask for approximate location and service type, with no financial account numbers or identifying documents.

Open `get_organization_details` for the strongest local match. Check the stated service area against the location, distinguish published eligibility from a confirmed qualification, and list cost, intake contact and hours when available. If the source omits a fee or required document, say it is unconfirmed. End with the most direct official next step. No application, donation or appointment is created by a search.
